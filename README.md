# MY FAVORITE SERIES

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is one of my favorite television series. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers. I enjoy watching this series because of its interesting story, amazing characters, and unexpected plot twists.

---

## About the Series

*Stranger Things* takes place in a fictional town called Hawkins, Indiana. The story follows a group of friends who discover mysterious supernatural events and a secret government laboratory.

> Friends don't lie.
